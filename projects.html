🔍 INFER

Satellite-based Synthetic Control: Prescribed Burn Evaluation

Embedding-informed donor selection improves causal inference for environmental policy. Shows that embedding-matched controls reduce pre-treatment error 49% (0.45→0.23) and reveal 4.4% wildfire mitigation effect (95% CI: [−5.1%, −3.7%]).

📊 Data: MODIS FIRMS, 3,966 treated pixels, 2003-2018 🔬 Method: Synthetic Control with Embeddings (PrithVi V2)

🗺️ TRANSFER

Spatial Policy Transportability: AWD in Japan

Vietnam's Alternate Wet Drying (AWD) water-saving policy evaluated for transfer to Japan. Water balance modeling + biophysical constraints reveal AWD feasible in 18% of Japan (vs. 45% Vietnam), concentrated in Kanto/Tohoku. Spatial fragmentation raises extension costs 3.8×.

🌾 Focus: Agricultural policy, spatial heterogeneity, institutional feasibility 📍 Geographies: Vietnam Mekong Delta → Japan paddy systems

📊 MODEL

Trade Policy Incidence: Vietnam–China HRC Tariffs

Vietnam's 23–28% anti-dumping tariffs on Chinese Hot-Rolled Coil steel. Firm-level analysis reveals policy incidence is inverted: domestic mills profit (duopoly control) while downstream factories lose (24% cost increase). 35% tariff exemptions create institutional capture; macro outcome: stagflation risk.

💼 Data: 105 months firm-level trade, supply-chain effects ⚙️ Method: Policy incidence, AD-AS modeling, stakeholder analysis

⚖️ DECIDE

Bayesian Model Comparison: Argentina Fiscal Policy

Which model best predicts Argentina's government wage spending response to tax revenue? Bayesian comparison across 6 models. Student-T cubic wins decisively (ELPD 289 vs. Normal Linear 125)—200 million times more likely. Heavy tails capture outliers; cubic captures non-linear thresholds.

📈 Data: 105 months government finance, 10 revenue streams (2016-2024) 🔬 Method: PyMC Bayesian inference, PSIS-LOO model validation
